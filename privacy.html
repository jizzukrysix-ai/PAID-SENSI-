Part 1: Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [VaultDrop] ("we", "us", "our") handles user information within the Telegram Mini App environment. We are committed to minimizing data collection and maintaining transparency about how we operate.

1. Information We Collect

We operate under the principle of data minimization. We only collect the technical data necessary to provide and manage our service:

  • **Telegram User ID:** We store your numeric Telegram ID and first name/username to identify your session and maintain your file-unlock progress across different slots.
  • **App Interaction Data:** We collect basic, anonymous event logs (such as which file slot is selected and whether a verification step was successfully completed). This is used for service optimization and ad completion analytics.
  • **Third-Party Data (Ad Networks):** Third-party advertising networks (like Adsgram) may automatically collect certain information when you interact with ads in our app, such as your IP address, device identifier, and ad interaction history, subject to their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Operate, maintain, and optimize the VaultDrop Mini App.
  • Verify your completion of required ad verification steps before releasing file access.
  • Analyze app usage trends to prevent fraudulent activity and automated abuse.

3. Data Security and Third Parties

Your data is secured using standard technical measures. We do not sell or share your personal ID details with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is shared only with:

  • **Service Providers:** Critical partners necessary for infrastructure and service delivery (e.g., our hosting provider Vercel/Netlify).
  • **Advertising Partners:** Our trusted ad networks (like Adsgram), who receive interaction data strictly to track successful ad views for compensation purposes.

Part 2: Terms of Use

By using the [vaultdrop] Telegram Mini App, you agree to abide by these Terms of Use. If you do not agree to these terms, do not use our service.

1. Description of Service

[vaultdrop] is a file retrieval directory. It allows users to browse file slots and obtain external download links (e.g., Google Drive, direct ZIP archives) after completing standard verification steps.

2. Monetization and Verification Flow

To keep the service free, VaultDrop uses a sponsored ad-verification model. We ensure compliance with ad network guidelines (such as Adsgram) by adhering to these rules:

  • **Manual Trigger:** Video ads will only be shown after a user explicitly clicks the verification button inside the file slot modal. Ads will **never** trigger automatically upon app open or slot selection.
  • **Required Completion:** Access to the final download link is strictly granted only after the full completion of the designated video ads and/or a mandatory timer pass.
  • **Anti-AdBlock Policy:** Users must not use tools designed to block advertisements. AdBlock usage may result in a non-functional experience.

3. External Content and Liability

[vaultdrop] serves as a directory only. We host information *about* the files, but the files themselves are hosted externally. While we strive to maintain functional links, we are not responsible for the content, security, or availability of any third-party links (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) provided through our service.
